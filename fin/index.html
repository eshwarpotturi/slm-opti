Details

What we did to the small model

Fine-tuning: Gemma 3 4B was trained for 36 minutes on a free GPU, on 1,026 solved questions from the benchmark's training split, to write the formula for the answer. Code then does the arithmetic and checks that every figure in the formula is printed in the report. Alone, the model rose from 54% to 59%. Paired with a second small model, the answers the two agree on are 92% correct, up from 86% for the pair without fine-tuning.

What we tried that did not help

Each of these was tried on Gemma 3 4B with 100 questions from the benchmark's separate development split. None beat the model alone, so none was used. The mistakes are mostly choosing the wrong row or year, not arithmetic. With 100 questions, a few points either way is within chance.
